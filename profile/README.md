# Welcome to Qliqy

**Be reachable. Stay invisible.**

Qliqy is a QR code you print and stick on a thing: whoever scans it can write to you without installing anything, and you reply from the app. Neither side sees the other's phone, email or name. One code per thing, and you can pause or replace any of them whenever you want.

## Who it is for

- **Owners.** A car, a door, a bike, a stall or a marketplace listing: stay reachable without handing out your number.
- **Visitors.** Scan the code, write a message, done. No account and no app.
